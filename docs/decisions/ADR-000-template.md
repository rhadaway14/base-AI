# ADR-000: Decision title

- **Status:** Proposed
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Deciders:**

## Context

What forces make this decision necessary? Include constraints and evidence, not just preferences.

## Decision

What are we choosing? Be specific enough that implementation and review can tell whether the decision is being followed.

## Alternatives considered

### Alternative A

Why it was credible, and why it was not chosen.

## Consequences

**Positive.** What gets easier or safer?

**Negative.** What complexity or cost are we accepting?

**Risks.** What could invalidate this decision, and how will we detect it?

## Verification

What code, test, metric, or operational check proves the decision remains true?
